# Independent Design Assessment and Capstone

## Objective

Develop authentic assessment evidence for `TASK-081` / `REQ-LEARN-003`. The goal is to determine whether a learner can solve a new visual communication problem independently, not whether they can replay a tutorial.

## Assessment design procedure

1. Start from `LEARNING_OUTCOMES.md` and the verified tool inventory. Define which PLOs and core tools are actually assessed.
2. Write a realistic new brief with audience, purpose, message, deliverables, constraints, and success criteria.
3. Require a short Discover/Define/Explore/Plan record, including at least two viable directions or alternatives and a reasoned selection.
4. Require independent execution in the appropriate Affinity studio/workspace. Do not give a click-by-click sequence that pre-decides every important choice.
5. Require a critique and iteration record: what did not work, what evidence or feedback identified it, and what changed.
6. Require an editable source file and appropriate final exports. Specify file formats and quality checks only when supported by the actual delivery context.
7. Require a concise design rationale and reflection, including limitations and possible next improvements.

## Common rubric

Use the seven dimensions in `LEARNING_OUTCOMES.md`:
- brief and audience;
- design reasoning;
- tool and workflow choice;
- craft and consistency;
- iteration and critique;
- delivery;
- reflection.

Score each from Level 1 to Level 4 using the published competency definitions. Define observable anchors for each score in the assessment-specific rubric; do not award points for effort, attendance, visual taste alone, or task completion without evidence.

## Graduation safeguards

- Every critical dimension must meet Level 3 (Independent execution) for the proposed pass standard.
- Critical failures cannot be averaged away.
- A familiar tutorial recreation cannot serve as the sole capstone.
- A learner must explain why the visual solution fits its audience and brief.
- Keep lesson completion, practice completion, assessed competency, and certification status separate in the product data and UI.
- The capstone and threshold are proposed standards until independent QA approves them.

## QA and closure

Have a reviewer who did not author the assessment check brief ambiguity, fairness, tool-scope validity, rubric observability, authenticity of transfer, deliverable feasibility, and scoring consistency. Retain anonymized exemplar evidence where permitted. Do not mark `TASK-081` complete or enable a graduation claim until track coverage, the assessment, and independent QA criteria are all satisfied.
